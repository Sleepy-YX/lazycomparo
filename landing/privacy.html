Privacy & terms

We don't want your data.

LazyComparo has no accounts, no sign-up, no newsletter, no analytics and no advertising trackers. There is no database of users because there are no users to put in it. This page explains what does happen when you load the site.

The short version

  • We ask you for nothing and store nothing about you on our servers.
  • Your shortlist and settings are saved in your own browser and never sent to us.
  • We set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Loading the site does contact a few third parties — hosting, fonts, code libraries and game cover art. They're listed below.

What's stored on your device

The comparison tools remember your choices using your browser's localStorage, so the site is useful when you come back. These stay on your device, are readable only by this site, and are never transmitted anywhere:

  • lc-games-shortlist — the games you've shortlisted
  • lc-games-priorities — how you weighted what matters to you
  • lc-games-budget — your budget slider position

Clearing your browser's site data for LazyComparo removes all of it, permanently and immediately. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so there is no copy for us to delete on request. If you use a private/incognito window, the site works and simply forgets you when the window closes.

What our servers see

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the standard request information needed to serve a page and defend against abuse — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not add our own logging on top of it, and we do not build profiles, sessions or identifiers from it.

The price endpoints run on Cloudflare's edge and call the stores server-side. That's a deliberate privacy property as well as a technical one: Steam, Epic, GOG and IsThereAnyDeal see our server asking about a game, not your browser — your IP is not passed on to them.

Children

The site is a general-audience buying-advice tool and is not directed at children. We knowingly collect nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your rights (PDPA)

LazyComparo is operated from Singapore and follows the Personal Data Protection Act. Because we collect no personal data and hold no user records, there is in practice nothing to access, correct, port or erase — but if you believe we hold something about you, or you have a privacy question, please raise it on GitHub and it will be answered.
